New reservation — manual entry

Commission above 12% is blocked (FIN-005). The booking cannot reach CONFIRMED until the Commercial Director approves in writing. An approval alert has been generated.

Agent sees net rate only (public − commission). The final client pays the public price; invoice is issued in the client's name. Commission voucher auto-generates at COMPLETED, payable 30 days post-cruise.

Group booking (OPS-008): same rates and conditions as FIT. Each cabin gets its own booking ID under one group; the lead guest is the group coordinator and single point of contact.
